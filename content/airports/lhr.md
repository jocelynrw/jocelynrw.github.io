Heathrow is a pretty massive airport, but it's easy to get around.  The Elizabeth Line runs from Terminal 2/3 to 4 and 5
in an alternating rhythym.  I had no trouble getting though security, but also they didn't announce my gate until half an hour prior to boarding.  They also set the boarding time for like an hour before take off, so there is a lot of cushion basically.
